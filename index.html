Curriculum of the Mind

Six Courses of Deep Transformation

10 DAYS · FOUNDATIONAL

The Breath (Ānāpānasati)

Start where the Buddha started. Ten days of pure observation — nothing to force, nothing to fix. Just this breath, exactly as it is.

FEATURED · 21 DAYS

The Four Absorptions (Jhāna)

From stillness to rapture. A gentle, structured guide into the four absorptions — where effort dissolves and joy becomes the ground beneath you.

14 DAYS · INSIGHT

Seeing Clearly (Vipassanā)

Looking at the mind until the mind stops lying. Learn to witness sensation without being pulled into the story of sensation.

7 DAYS · FOCUS

Total Object (Kasiṇa)

Single-point absorption through external elements — earth, water, fire, and pure light. The ancient method of unwavering focus.

14 DAYS · COMPASSION

Loving Without Limit (Mettā)

The antidote to fear, resentment, and isolation. Radiate unconditional goodwill to all living beings without distinction or barrier.

21 DAYS · MINDFULNESS

The Noting Method

Labeling phenomena as they arise: rising, falling, hearing, thinking. A precise discipline from the Mahāsī Sayādaw tradition.
